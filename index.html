28 Years of Culinary Heritage

Culinary Excellence in the Heart of Cork

Since 1998, Bombay Palace has brought the rich, diverse culinary heritage of India straight to Cork City. Our experienced chefs prepare each dish using time-honoured techniques, freshly ground spice blends, and locally sourced Irish ingredients.

Whether you are dining in with family, hosting a Christmas gathering, or celebrating a milestone occasion, we welcome you with the same warmth and royal hospitality that Cork has loved for nearly three decades.
