Culinary Excellence

Bombay Palace brings the rich, diverse culinary heritage of India straight to the heart of Cork City. Our experienced chefs prepare each dish using traditional techniques, freshly ground spice blends, and locally sourced ingredients.

Whether you are dining in with family or celebrating a special occasion, we offer an inviting atmosphere filled with warmth and hospitality.
