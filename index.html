About Us

Experience the magic of authentic Indian dining in Cork

Culinary Excellence in the Heart of Cork

Bombay Palace brings the rich, diverse culinary heritage of India straight to Cork City. Our experienced chefs prepare each dish using traditional techniques, freshly ground spice blends, and locally sourced ingredients.

Whether you are dining in with family or celebrating a special occasion, we offer an inviting atmosphere filled with warmth and hospitality.

Authentic Recipes Crafted with hand-selected Indian spices and fresh ingredients.
Warm Hospitality An inviting ambiance perfect for family gatherings & celebrations.
